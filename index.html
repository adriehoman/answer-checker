Individual Support Learning Assistant

Study and assessment-support assistant
❓ How to use your assistant

Open a topic to read it. Press Read this to me to hear it.

👋 Getting started
  1. Type the access code from your teacher and press Start.
  2. Choose your Unit, then your Assessment.
  3. Choose the Question. If the question has parts, choose the Question part.
  4. Look at the question in the box. Is it the same as your assessment? Press Yes – this is my question. If it looks different, press My question looks different and paste your question.
✍️ Checking your answer
  1. Write your answer in the answer box.
  2. Press Check my answer.
  3. BodE checks the meaning of your answer, not only the words. You can change your answer and check again.

BodE does not give you the answer. Your teacher still marks your assessment.

🎨 What the colours mean
  • 🟢 Green: your answer seems to cover what the question asks.
  • 🟠 Orange: a good start. Part of the answer is still missing. Read the tips, then add more.
  • 🔴 Red: your answer does not answer the question yet. Read the question again, or ask for a hint.
  • 🙂 BodE says: that was not a real answer yet. Try writing your own ideas.
💡 Hints and references
  • Orange or red? Press Give me a hint. Press it again for a second hint.
  • Press Show the reference to see where to find the information: the Nearpod or Genially slide, the Learner Guide page, or a part of your assessment.
  • Answers you learned from Nearpod and the class presentations are accepted. Write them in your own words.
  • After two tries, ask your teacher for help.
📖 Help with words and questions
  • Explain this question more simply: BodE tells you what the question is asking, in easy English.
  • Words with a dotted line in the question: click the word to see what it means.
  • Word help for this unit: a list of the important words for the unit.
  • Pick a question for me: BodE chooses a question for you to practise.
🔊 Voice mode (BodE reads to you)
  1. Press Voice mode: Off to turn it on. BodE will talk to you.
  2. BodE reads your feedback, hints and references out loud. Press Read again to hear it again.
  3. Press Read the question to me to hear the question.
  4. In class, please use headphones.
  5. To turn it off, press Voice mode: On.
🎤 Talking instead of typing
  1. Turn on Voice mode. Then press Speak my answer. If the browser asks, press Allow.
  2. Wait one second, then speak slowly and clearly. Press Stop speaking when you finish.
  3. Read the words in the box. Fix any wrong words by typing. Then press Check my answer.

No microphone button? On Windows, press the Windows key + H to talk in any box.

Privacy: When you use the microphone, your voice goes to Google (Chrome) or Microsoft (Edge) to change it into words. Do not say names or personal details.

⚙️ Settings
  • Choose BodE's voice and reading speed.
  • Choose the English accent closest to yours. It helps the microphone understand you.
  • Make the text bigger, or turn on high contrast (darker text).
  • Your settings stay on this computer only. Press Reset settings to go back to normal.
🙂 When BodE gives a friendly reply

If you type "I don't know", random letters, or ask for the answer, BodE gives you a friendly tip instead of checking. Nothing is checked. Try writing what you already know, even one idea. Press Show the reference to find where to look.

💬 Telling your teacher about a problem
  1. Open Tell your teacher about a problem or an idea at the top of the page.
  2. Choose if your message is about your question or about the whole program.
  3. Choose what happened, write a short message, and press Send to my teacher.

Use it when BodE's colour looks wrong, when something is confusing, or when something does not work. Your teacher reads the messages later. BodE does not answer them. If you need help now, ask your teacher in class.

🔒 Privacy
  • Do not type your name, student number, email, or any personal or health information.
  • To improve this tool, your answer, the question, the result and the attempt number are kept for up to 90 days, without your name. Messages you send to your teacher are kept the same way.
  • Your sign-in ends when you close the browser, or after 8 hours. Press Log out when you finish on a shared computer.
🧑‍🏫 If something goes wrong
  • Question check needed: choose the right question, or paste your question wording.
  • Cannot connect: check your internet and try again.
  • Very busy today: try again later or tomorrow.
  • Still stuck? Ask your teacher.
⚙️ Settings
Choose the accent closest to yours. It helps the microphone understand you. If yours is not listed, try Indian English or Australian English.

Your settings are saved in this browser on this computer only. Nothing is sent to BodE's server.

💬 Tell your teacher about a problem or an idea

Did BodE's colour look wrong? Was something confusing? Did something not work? Do you have an idea? Write it here. Your teacher reads the messages to make BodE better. BodE does not answer them.

🔒 Privacy: Do not write your name or personal details. Your message is saved without your name for up to 90 days. If it is about a question, the answer in your answer box and BodE's colour are sent with it, so your teacher can see what happened.

🧪 Test mode: checks and messages from this page are saved as tests, not as student answers.
